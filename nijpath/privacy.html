Privacy Policy

This policy explains what NijPath Browser stores on your device and what information may be processed when you browse, use ads, or connect through your own VPN configuration.

Private mode controls data stored by this browser.

It does not make you anonymous to websites, a network provider, a VPN server, or advertising providers. Ads may still appear in the Store edition while Private mode is on.

1. Who operates NijPath Browser

NijPath Browser is provided by H-VNCareer (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Store app com.tt.browser.tw and, where applicable, its separate Premium edition com.tt.browser.tw.premium. Contact us about privacy at long123v123@gmail.com. NijPath Browser does not require an app account.

2. Data stored on your device

Standard browsing stores open-tab URLs and titles, history, bookmarks, download records, preferences, and website data on your device. Android WebView also maintains cookies, cache, and other site storage. Downloaded files are managed by Android and can remain on your device until you remove them.

App Lock can use your device's screen lock. If you set an app-specific six-digit PIN, NijPath Browser stores a salted verification value and attempt counter in app-private storage, not the plaintext PIN. We do not operate a browser-history sync service or upload your local history, bookmarks, or PIN verifier to our own server as part of normal browsing.

3. Private sessions

Where supported by the installed Android WebView, Private tabs use a separate browser profile. Private visits are not added to Standard history. Ending a Private session closes its tabs and clears that profile's cookies and site data. The app also discards old Private profiles after an interrupted session when it next starts. Downloads are unavailable from Private tabs.

Private mode cannot erase information already received by a website, search engine, internet provider, VPN server, or advertising provider. Data you choose to send to a website is subject to that website's policy.

4. Optional browser-only VPN

If you import a WireGuard client configuration, NijPath Browser stores it, including the client private key, in app-private storage on your device. The app does not supply a VPN server or subscription. Android asks for VPN permission before connection. When connected, the tunnel is configured to carry NijPath Browser traffic only, including content and ads shown inside the app; other apps are outside its scope.

The server in your configuration and its operator may receive your device IP address, connection times, destinations, DNS requests, and any traffic not separately protected by HTTPS. Their privacy practices are independent of ours. We do not promise that a third-party server keeps no logs. A VPN does not guarantee anonymity or protect data that you intentionally send to a website. You can disconnect and remove the configuration in VPN settings.

5. Website permissions

A website may request camera, microphone, or approximate location access. NijPath Browser asks for the relevant Android and site permissions when needed. You can deny or change a permission in Browser settings or Android settings. A website you authorize may receive the resulting data and process it under its own policy. NijPath Browser does not use these permissions in the background to record you.

6. Advertising, analytics, and configuration

The Store edition contains advertising SDKs and may display ads, including in Private mode. It integrates Google AdMob and mediation libraries for Meta Audience Network, InMobi, Pangle, and AppLovin. Providers that are enabled and serve or measure ads may process IP address, IP-derived approximate location, advertising or app identifiers, app and ad interactions, device information, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. A bundled provider does not necessarily serve an ad in every session. The Premium edition disables in-app ads.

The app also includes Firebase Analytics and Remote Config libraries. When a NijPath Firebase client is configured and these services are enabled, they may process app-instance identifiers, app events, and technical information to measure app usage and deliver configuration. We do not intentionally send your local browsing history, bookmarks, VPN private key, or app-lock PIN verifier to advertising or analytics SDKs. Consent choices and provider settings may affect ad personalization and analytics.

See the providers' notices: Google, Firebase, Meta, InMobi, Pangle, and AppLovin.

7. Websites and external content

Searches and page requests go to the search engine or website you choose. Home shortcuts lead to third-party sites. The Short Drama section may load YouTube thumbnails and open selected videos in YouTube or another app. These services receive requests and may collect data under their own policies. We do not control third-party website cookies, content, or security.

These legal pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and request information for hosting and security. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Standard history, tabs, bookmarks, preferences, and download records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Browser settings allow you to clear selected history, cookies, website data, and cache. Ending a Private session removes its local tabs and site data. Removing a VPN profile deletes its local configuration. Downloaded files outside app-private storage may require separate deletion. Android backups, if enabled by your device, are governed by your backup settings; NijPath Browser currently disables its own Android app-data backup.

Deleting local data does not delete information already received by websites, VPN operators, ad providers, or analytics providers; their retention periods follow their own policies. To ask about information we control or exercise applicable privacy rights, email long123v123@gmail.com. There is no NijPath Browser account to delete.

9. Security and processing location

App-private files are protected by Android's app sandbox. App Lock uses your device authentication or a locally stored PIN verifier. HTTPS protects connections to HTTPS websites; the optional WireGuard tunnel encrypts traffic to the configured endpoint. No measure guarantees absolute security or anonymity. Advertising, analytics, and external content providers may process information in other countries.

10. Children

NijPath Browser is a general-audience browser and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Contact and policy changes

Privacy questions: long123v123@gmail.com. We may update this policy when features, SDKs, or data practices change. The current version and effective date will remain available here. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.

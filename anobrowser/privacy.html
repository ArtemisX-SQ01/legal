Privacy Policy

AnoBrowser is a browser with separate Private sessions, optional App Lock, advertising, and an optional VPN connection. This policy explains what those features do and what data may leave your device.

Private means local session control.

It does not make you anonymous to websites, a network provider, a VPN server, or advertising providers. Ads may appear while Private is on.

1. Who operates AnoBrowser

AnoBrowser is provided by H-VNCareer (“we”, “us”). You can contact us about privacy at long123v123@gmail.com. The app does not require an AnoBrowser account.

2. Data stored on your device

In Standard browsing, the app stores open-tab URLs and titles, browsing history, bookmarks, download records, preferences, and site data on your device. Android WebView also stores cookies, cache, and other website data. Downloads are managed by Android and may remain on your device until you remove them.

We do not run a browser-history sync service. The app does not send your local history or bookmarks to our own server as part of normal browsing. Websites you visit still receive your requests and may collect data under their own policies.

3. Private sessions

Where supported by the installed Android WebView, Private tabs use a separate browser profile. Private visits are not added to AnoBrowser's Standard history. Ending a Private session closes its tabs and clears its profile's cookies and website data. The app also discards old Private profiles after an interrupted session when it next starts. Downloads from Private tabs are currently unavailable.

Private mode does not prevent a website, internet provider, VPN server, or advertising SDK from processing network or device data. Data you deliberately send to a website remains subject to that website's policy.

4. Optional VPN

If you import a VPN configuration and enable VPN, AnoBrowser stores that configuration, including its client private key, in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to our ad or analytics services. The Android VPN permission is requested before connection. The VPN routes AnoBrowser traffic only; other apps continue using their normal network connection.

The server named in your imported configuration handles AnoBrowser's VPN traffic. Its operator may be able to observe your device IP address, connection times, destinations or DNS requests, and any traffic that is not separately encrypted. HTTPS protects website content independently of the VPN. For VPN servers operated by us: we do not store VPN activity or connection logs, including browsing activity, DNS requests, source IP addresses, or connection times. This no-logs policy applies to our VPN servers; a third-party server supplied through your own configuration may follow a different policy.

5. Website permissions

A website may request camera, microphone, or approximate location access. AnoBrowser asks for the relevant Android permission and site access when needed; you can deny the request or block the permission in Browser settings. If you grant access, the website may receive the resulting data. AnoBrowser does not use these permissions in the background to record you.

6. Advertising, analytics, and configuration

AnoBrowser displays ads. It integrates Google AdMob and may use mediation providers including Meta Audience Network, InMobi, Pangle, and AppLovin. These providers may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising or app identifiers, ad and app interactions, and device or diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Their handling is governed by their own policies and applicable consent choices. Private mode does not turn off in-app ads.

The app also includes Firebase Analytics, which may collect automatically generated app-usage events and identifiers, and Firebase Remote Config, which delivers app and ad configuration. We use this information to operate, measure, and improve AnoBrowser. We do not intentionally pass your local browsing history, bookmarks, or VPN private key to these SDKs.

For more about these providers, see Google's Privacy Policy, Meta's Privacy Policy, InMobi's Privacy Policy, Pangle's Privacy Policy, and AppLovin's Privacy Policy.

7. Websites and external content

Searches and pages you open are sent to the search engine or website you choose. Home shortcuts lead to third-party sites. The Short Drama section loads thumbnail images from YouTube and opens selected videos using an external app or browser. Those services may collect data under their own policies. We do not control third-party websites, their cookies, or their content.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security when this page is opened. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

8. Retention and your choices

Standard history, tabs, bookmarks, and download records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Browser settings let you clear selected history, cookies, website data, and cache. Ending a Private session removes its tabs and site data, but does not erase data a website, ad provider, or VPN operator already received. Removing the app deletes app-private data; files managed outside that storage may need separate deletion.

Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies. To ask us about data we control, or to exercise applicable privacy rights, contact long123v123@gmail.com. There is no AnoBrowser account to delete.

9. Security and data location

App-private files are protected by Android's app sandbox. App Lock, if enabled, uses your device's authentication; AnoBrowser does not receive your PIN, password, or biometric template. Network requests to HTTPS websites are encrypted by HTTPS, and the optional VPN encrypts traffic to its configured endpoint. No security measure can guarantee anonymity or absolute protection. Advertising and analytics providers may process data in other countries.

10. Children

AnoBrowser is a general-audience browser and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal data directly to us, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Contact and policy changes

Privacy questions: long123v123@gmail.com. We may update this policy when features, SDKs, or data practices change. The current version and its effective date will remain available on this page.
